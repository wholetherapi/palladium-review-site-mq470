---
title: "Halcyon Drift: Drift-Aware Scheduling for Shared Accelerator Pools"
kind: "conference-paper"
announced_at: "2026-06-15"
venue: "Symposium on Learned Systems 2027 (SLS '27)"
---

Shared accelerator pools accumulate clock and power drift over long campaigns, and global barriers make the drift visible only when a job already misses its deadline. Halcyon Drift estimates drift per pool and rebalances work continuously. The paper reports a 19% reduction in p99 job latency on a 64-accelerator cluster.
