---
title: "Cascade Routing for Adaptive Computation"
kind: "conference-paper"
announced_at: "2026-09-14"
venue: "International Conference on Adaptive Computation 2027 (ICAC '27)"
---

Cascade routing sends easy inputs through a small first stage and defers only ambiguous cases to a larger model, with a learned gate calibrated on a held-out split. The evaluation covers three public reasoning suites and reports a 2.1x throughput gain at matched accuracy.
